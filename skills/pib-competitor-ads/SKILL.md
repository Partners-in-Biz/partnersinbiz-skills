# PiB Competitor Ads

Monitor competitors' active ad campaigns to spot trends in hooks, messaging, and creative angles that can inform our own strategy.

## Purpose

See what competitors run. Inform our campaign hooks, audience angles, creative directions, and positioning by understanding the competitive landscape.

## Source

Meta Ad Library (public). Available at: facebook.com/ads/library

## Status: DEFERRED

This skill is **DEFERRED** until the T6 tool exists.

**Until T6 lands:**

- Do **not** scrape competitor sites, Ad Library, or any platform (no Selenium, Cheerio, Playwright, puppeteer, or custom scripts).
- Do **not** invent a procedure or tool name (e.g. "use fetch_ads_library" or "query T6").
- **Only** manual viewing of the public Meta Ad Library by a human is allowed.
- Any findings from manual viewing are noted as **unverified**.

**When T6 arrives:**

- This skill will be updated with a real procedure.
- Automated ad intelligence will be available to agents.

## What T6 will enable

When the T6 tool ships, this skill will document:

- Querying competitor ads by business name, category, or keyword.
- Filtering by platform, date range, and ad status.
- Extracting hooks, visuals, copy, and engagement metrics.
- Outputting findings in a structured format for strategy briefs.

Until then, stay manual.
